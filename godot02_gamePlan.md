## Game Plan (fill out on planning day)

**Game name:** Grand Theft Data Center

**Platformer or RPG?**  Platformer

**Answer:** We plan to create a platformer game centered around retrieving a stolen car.


**What is your game about?** Who is the player, and where are they?

**Answer:** Somebdy stole your car and blew up a data center with it, now youre trying to get the car back. The player plays as Dylan who is trying to recover his stolen car which was stolen by Tyler.


**What does the player do?** (Run and jump? Explore? Collect things? Avoid things?)

**Answer:**The player can complete levels by running on foot, driving, or electric scooter. The player can collect money as collectibles to unlock items in the game. The player has to avoid bad drivers, the elderly, and Tyler himself.


**What are the controls?**

**Answer:** Arrow keys and WASD to move, space to hit things, E to pickup things


**How do you win?**

**Answer:** By getting your car back and completing the levels.


**How do you lose?**

**Answer:** Getting stuck behind the elderly, getting struck by bad drivers, or falling off the terrain.

**Where will your sound come from?** (Brackeys sounds, found online, or made ourselves)

**Answer:**
We will find our sounds online.

**Draw or describe your first level or map.** What's at the start, the middle, and the end?

**Answer:**
Our first level is at Dylan's garage, where his car went missing. He has to navigate his neighborhood to find clues to the missing whereabouts of his car. While doing so, the player can collect money and must avoid the elderly people on the sidewalk by either mario stomping them, or moving around them.
